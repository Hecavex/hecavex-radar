# Data licensing and attribution

The Apache License 2.0 covers the HECAVEX Radar software and original documentation. It does not relicense third-party feed records, trademarks, screenshots, or source material.

Published records retain their source names and the snapshot includes each source homepage. Deployers and downstream users are responsible for the current access, attribution, redistribution, and rate-limit terms of every source they enable.

## Source-specific notes

- **PhishTank:** data is provided under PhishTank's current Terms of Use and stated Creative Commons terms. Keep PhishTank attribution and review those terms before redistribution.
- **Certificate Transparency / CertStream:** certificate observations are public CT facts. The included matcher and collector are Apache-2.0; third-party server and CT-log software retain their own licenses.
- **VMRay and OpenPhish:** disabled by default. Enabling either source requires explicit terms acceptance by the deployer. Their data is not relicensed here.
- **HECAVEX export:** only observations separately approved for public release may enter this repository. The exporter must not expose private history, proprietary evidence, credentials, or personal data.
- **Screenshots:** remain subject to the screenshot provider and depicted-site rights. Only allow-listed HTTPS providers may be published.

Brand names and domains in `data/brands-lt.json` identify their owners and do not imply endorsement. Each registry entry must cite an authoritative public source.

To report a false positive, sensitive path value, attribution problem, or removal request, email [info@hecavex.com](mailto:info@hecavex.com).
